Interactive System Architecture

Why & How KharifShield Works — Click Any Section to Expand

Tap cards below to read full explanation

Standard weather apps only show raw rainfall numbers (like "14 mm tomorrow"), leaving the farmer to guess whether to switch on the borewell. Conversely, static agricultural pamphlets assume unlimited groundwater and recommend fixed weekly watering.

KharifShield bridges that gap: it combines your borewell water reserve (25%–100% or custom capacity), your soil's water-holding category (Red, Mixed, or Black), your crop's growth stage, and live 3-day district rainfall to give one clear, actionable decision today—prioritizing scarce water for the most sensitive cob-forming weeks rather than splitting water evenly across the season.

Maize does not need equal water every week. According to FAO Irrigation & Drainage Paper 33, stage sensitivity factors differ sharply: Vegetative (0.40), Flowering (1.50), Yield Formation (0.50), and Ripening (0.20), with a separate whole-season Ky = 1.25.

Our planner intentionally holds back early water—encouraging deeper root growth—and reserves 55% to 65% of scarce borewell water for Weeks 5–10, adjusting guidance using approximate soil presets (5–6 days for fast-draining Red soil vs. 9–11 days for moisture-retaining Black soil, pending local field calibration).

In-field IoT soil probes involve upfront hardware costs, battery upkeep, and field maintenance, and can get damaged during ploughing—creating practical barriers for many smallholder farmers in Telangana.

KharifShield requires zero field hardware in a mobile browser (loading UI styling and fonts from CDN assets on first load), connects to live Open-Meteo weather, geocoding, and ISRIC SoilGrids APIs with clean sample/district fallbacks when connectivity drops, and reads every instruction aloud in plain Telugu and English.

Every time you check your plan, KharifShield queries live 3-day precipitation from Open-Meteo for your resolved coordinates. If ≥ 12 mm of rain is forecast, an illustrative 3-day UI heuristic suggests pausing pumping (full daily soil-water balance integration pending).

When high rainfall (>30 mm in 3 days) is forecast—or in our 68 mm UI demo test—the app advises keeping pumps off and clearing field drainage channels because standing water for over 24 hours can suffocate maize roots.

Methodology & Honest Scope

Scientific Foundation & Honest Limits

  • FAO-56 / FAO-33 Water Model: Vegetative (0.40), Flowering (1.50), Yield Formation (0.50), Ripening (0.20); separate whole-season Ky = 1.25.
  • Open-Meteo Live Weather: Fetches 3-day precipitation for resolved coordinates with a clearly labeled sample weather fallback if offline.
  • Honest Limits: Uses SoilGrids / 3 soil presets (Red/Mixed/Black), heuristic planning scores, and static district placeholders rather than plot lab assays or field-validated yield predictions.
